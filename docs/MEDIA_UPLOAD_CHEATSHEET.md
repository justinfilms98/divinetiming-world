# Media upload cheat sheet — Divine Timing

For Lex and Laurance. Use these until automatic optimization is built; after that the site will
shrink files for you, but these settings will still give the best-looking results.

## Space you have right now

- **Largest single file: 50 MB.** Bigger files will be refused.
- **Total space: 1 GB**, about **665 MB free** today.
- The bigger the hero videos, the slower the homepage loads on phones and the faster the monthly
  data allowance runs out. Smaller is better.

## Homepage hero videos (up to 3)

| | Use this |
|---|---|
| Format | **MP4 (H.264)** |
| Size | **1920 × 1080 (1080p)**, landscape. Don't upload 4K or 1440p. |
| Length | **8–15 seconds**, ideally a smooth loop |
| Frame rate | 24, 25 or 30 fps (not 60) |
| Sound | **Remove the audio** (it plays muted anyway) |
| File size | **Aim for 4–6 MB per clip, never above 10 MB** |
| Quality setting | About 4–5 Mbit/s, or HandBrake preset "Fast 1080p30" with RF 23–24 |

**Phones show only the middle third of the video.** On a phone the landscape clip is cropped to a
tall slice. Keep faces, the logo moment and any important action in the **centre third**, and avoid
text near the left and right edges. After uploading, check the phone preview and adjust the focal
point if needed.

Pick a calm first frame: it is what people see while the video loads.

## Photos

| Use | Shape | Size | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero / page banners | Landscape, 3:2 or 16:9 | 2400–3000 px wide | JPEG, under 3 MB |
| Gallery / collections | Any shape (shown uncropped) | 2000–2500 px on the long side | JPEG, under 2 MB |
| Events | 3:2 landscape or 4:5 portrait | 2000 px on the long side | JPEG, under 2 MB |
| Shop products | **Square 1:1**, plain background | 2000 × 2000 px | JPEG, under 2 MB |
| Press kit downloads | Full resolution | up to 4000 px | JPEG, under 10 MB |

- Export in **sRGB** colour.
- iPhone photos: HEIC files may not display everywhere. Use **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most
  Compatible**, or export as JPEG before uploading.

## Other videos (library, events, galleries)

- MP4 (H.264), 1080p, **under 45 MB**, ideally under 60 seconds.
- Anything longer (sets, interviews, music videos) → **upload to YouTube** and add it under
  *Media → YouTube Videos*. YouTube handles quality and data for free.

## Quick export recipes

- **iPhone camera:** Settings → Camera → Record Video → **1080p at 30 fps**; Formats → **Most Compatible**.
- **CapCut / Premiere / DaVinci Resolve:** H.264, 1920×1080, 30 fps, bitrate ~5 Mbit/s (VBR), audio off for hero clips.
- **HandBrake (free):** preset *Fast 1080p30* → Video: RF 23 → Audio tab: remove track → Start.

## Before you publish

1. New events, collections, products and releases start as **Drafts**. Nothing is public until you choose **Published**.
2. Check the phone and desktop previews, and the focal point.
3. Publish.
